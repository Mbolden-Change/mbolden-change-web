How it works

You'll follow a household through a series of federal policy cuts. At each stage, you'll face the impossible tradeoffs real families face when benefits are reduced. Every choice has consequences.

Household stability measures a family's ability to meet basic needs — food, housing, healthcare, childcare. Each household starts with a stability score based on their income, benefits, and expenses. As policy changes take effect, you'll watch that stability erode.

The stability scale
75%+Stable
50–74%One crisis away
25–49%Basic needs at risk
Below 25%Crisis

There are no good options — only less bad ones.
