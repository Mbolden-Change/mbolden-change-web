Our model

How we partner

We treat communities as co-creators, and bold change as a mandate. We co-create solutions with trusted partners and movement builders, and we resource them.

Listen

Community partners and the families they serve set the agenda. We co-design the work with the people who live it.

Build and strengthen

Alongside local partners, we meet urgent needs, strengthen the organizations doing the work, and grow what works.

Advocate
